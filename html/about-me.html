About me

My background: I started building worlds and modding in Minecraft. What began as a game quickly became a passion for creation and logic. That curiosity about 'how things work' led me to the world of programming, computers and the Programming course at Thomas More.

What I do: I am currently learning the ins and outs of the programming trade. I'm not afraid to try new things. I prefer to break things first, only to fix them myself afterward.

My Style: As you can see on this website, I like a clean, technical look. The cats? Those are a nod to programmer culture (ASCII art) and my own pets who often use my keyboard as a bed. Cozy, but professional.
